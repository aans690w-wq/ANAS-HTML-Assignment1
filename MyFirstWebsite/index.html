Masarak

Masarak is an Android application designed to serve as a tourism and cultural guide to the historical, archaeological, and tourist landmarks of the Gaza Strip. It helps users discover the history and cultural heritage of Gaza through organized information and images.

Key Features

  • Displaying famous landmarks in Gaza.
  • Showing images and detailed information about each landmark.
  • Providing historical backgrounds and descriptions.
  • Helping users explore Palestinian cultural heritage.

Technologies Used: Java, Android Studio

Project Type: Android Application Development

Gym Chef

Gym Chef is a fitness and nutrition application that combines healthy cooking, sports nutrition, and fitness progress tracking. It helps users choose meals that match their fitness goals, understand their nutritional values, and monitor their physical progress.

Key Features:

  • Healthy Recipes and Meal Details
  • Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions
  • Nutritional Information
  • Daily Nutrition Tracking
  • Weight and Progress Tracking

Technologies Used: Figma

Project Type: UI/UX Design
